PART 1: ACADEMICS
#####################################

ACADEMIC CALENDAR
- Odd semester: July to December. Even semester: January to May.
- Mid-semester exams: 8th week of each semester. End-semester exams: last two weeks of the semester.
- Hall tickets are on the student portal 7 days before exams (needs 75% attendance and no pending fees).
- Results are declared within 30 days after the last exam on the student portal.

RULES
- Minimum attendance: 75% in each subject. Students with 65% to 74% can apply for condonation (Rs 300 fee, valid reason needed). Below 65% students cannot sit for exams.
- Passing criteria: minimum 40% in each subject, with at least 35% in the end-semester exam.
- Re-evaluation: apply at the examination cell within 10 days of result, Rs 500 per subject.
- Missed an exam due to illness: submit a medical certificate within 3 days to the examination cell.
- Grading: 10-point CGPA. 90%+ is grade O (10), 80-89% is A+ (9), 70-79% is A (8), 60-69% is B+ (7), 50-59% is B (6), 40-49% is C (5).

ACADEMIC SUPPORT AVAILABLE
- Faculty mentor: every student is assigned a faculty mentor. Meet them at least once a month.
- Doubt-clearing sessions: every Wednesday and Friday, 4:00 PM to 5:00 PM, in the department seminar room.
- Remedial classes for weak students: announced by each department after mid-semester results.
- Peer tutoring: toppers of senior batches help juniors. Register with the Academic Cell, Room 110, Admin Block.
- Library: open 8 AM to 8 PM on weekdays, 9 AM to 2 PM on Saturday. Borrow 3 books for 14 days. E-books and journals available on the student portal.
- Computer labs: open until 6 PM. Extra lab hours can be booked for project work with the lab in-charge.
- Student portal: portal.abc-college.example. Username is enrollment number, default password is date of birth (DDMMYYYY).

PROJECTS AND INTERNSHIPS
- Mini project in 5th semester and major project in 8th semester. Groups of up to 4 students. Choose a guide in the first month.
- Internships: register with the Training and Placement Cell (Room 301, Admin Block). Summer internships after 4th and 6th semester earn credits.

PART 2: CO-CURRICULAR ACTIVITIES
#####################################

CLUBS (join by contacting the faculty coordinator or attending the orientation in the first month)
- Coding Club: weekly coding practice on Saturdays 10 AM, hackathon training, competitive programming. Coordinator: Dr. Sharma, Computer Block.
- Robotics Club: builds robots and drones, participates in national robotics contests. Meets Tuesdays and Thursdays 4 PM in the Robotics Lab.
- Cultural Club: dance, music, drama and fine arts. Practice in the Seminar Hall, evenings.
- Literary Society: debates, quizzes, creative writing, public speaking, MUN. Meets Mondays 3:30 PM, Library hall.

- Entrepreneurship Cell (E-Cell): startup ideas, pitch contests, mentoring by alumni.
- Photography and Media Club: photography, video editing, college magazine.

ANNUAL EVENTS
- Tech Fest "TechNova": February, includes hackathon, coding contests, robotics challenge, paper presentations.
- Cultural Fest "Rangtarang": March, includes dance, music, drama and fashion show.
- Annual Sports Meet: January.
- Fresher's Welcome: August. Farewell: May.
- Registration for events opens on the student portal and notice board about 3 weeks before each event.

BENEFITS AND CREDITS
- Activity points: students need at least 100 activity points over 4 years to graduate. Clubs, events, volunteering, certifications and internships all earn points.
- Certificates: participation, winner and organiser certificates are issued by the Student Affairs Office, Room 120, Admin Block, within 2 weeks of the event.
- Leadership roles: student council elections are held every August. Club head, secretary and event coordinator roles are selected by faculty coordinators.
- Funding: the college supports travel and registration fees for students who represent the college at external competitions. Apply to the Student Affairs Office with the event details at least 10 days before.

CERTIFICATIONS AND SKILLS
- The college offers free access to online learning platforms for coding, data science and communication skills through the Training and Placement Cell.
- Recommended certifications by branch: Computer Science (cloud, web development, data analytics), Electronics (embedded systems, IoT), Mechanical (CAD, automation), Civil (AutoCAD, surveying).
